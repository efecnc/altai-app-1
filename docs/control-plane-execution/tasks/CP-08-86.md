# CP-08-86 — LongHorizon conformance discovery

- **Package:** 081 — LongHorizon codebase (PR 1)
- **Status:** accepted — `altaidevorg/altai-app` PR #834 (merge
  `f8b8b948`, 2026-08-20) recorded the pinned source, provenance decision,
  and deterministic benchmark contract
- **Depends on:** 035 (event translation), 044 (liveness and recovery),
  045 (evidence), and CP-08-85 (the completed real downstream plane)

## Outcome

Produce a reviewable adopt/defer decision for LongHorizon mechanisms before
any source is copied, enabled, or treated as an ALTAI authority. The discovery
must identify the exact upstream revision, license and provenance; map each
candidate mechanism to ALTAI's canonical Work/Attempt/Activity/Evidence
contracts; and define a reproducible conformance benchmark for long-running
recovery and evaluation behavior.

The benchmark must cover at least these observable contracts:

- a worker interruption and reattachment without a duplicate attempt or lost
  lease;
- translated lifecycle activity remaining queryable and correlated to its Work
  item;
- durable evidence sufficient to explain the recovery/evaluation outcome; and
- a deterministic replay or explicit reason that replay is outside the
  candidate's boundary.

## Scope

- Locate the authoritative LongHorizon repository and pin the inspected
  revision, license, and upstream history.
- Inventory only mechanisms relevant to recovery, evaluation, replay, and
  evidence. Record their inputs, outputs, state ownership, side effects, and
  proposed adapter boundary.
- Add a conformance matrix and a decision record stating **adopt**, **defer**,
  or **reject** for every candidate. "Useful" or "similar" is not evidence.
- No runtime integration, vendored source, new authority path, or feature flag
  is in scope for this task.

The pinned source inventory, decision, and benchmark fixture are recorded in
`LONGHORIZON_CONFORMANCE_DISCOVERY.md`.

## Evidence

- The authoritative repository, immutable inspected revision, and MIT license
  blob are recorded in the discovery record.
- Every candidate mechanism has an explicit adopt/defer/reject decision and
  an ALTAI ownership boundary.
- `LH-081-recovery-evidence-v1` has concrete actions and deterministic
  pass/fail observations; CP-08-87 implements the fixture against ALTAI's
  real-plane wiring without importing an external runtime.

## Acceptance

- The authoritative source and revision are independently reproducible, with
  license/provenance recorded.
- The matrix maps every proposed behavior to an existing ALTAI contract or
  declares the missing contract and its risk.
- The benchmark is executable or has a concrete fixture, expected observations,
  and deterministic pass/fail criteria suitable for a follow-up PR.
- A decision record is reviewed with a bounded next action; code adoption may
  start only when that decision explicitly authorizes it.

## Handoff

The follow-up task must implement only the mechanisms explicitly approved by
the decision record and run the benchmark against the production control-plane
contracts. It must not replace ALTAI Work, Attempt, lease, activity, or
evidence ownership.
